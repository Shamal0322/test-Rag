# Sample Company Policy

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Team leads approve remote work schedules every quarter.

## Security Training

All employees must complete the annual security awareness training by March 31. New employees must complete onboarding security training within 14 days after their start date.

## Equipment

The company provides one laptop, one charger, and one external display for full-time employees. Lost equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

## Travel

Business travel requires manager approval before booking. Economy class is required for flights under six hours.
