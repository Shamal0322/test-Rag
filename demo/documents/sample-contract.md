# Sample Service Contract

## Parties

This sample service contract is between Example GmbH and Demo Client AG.

## Term

The contract starts on January 1, 2026 and ends on December 31, 2026 unless renewed in writing.

## Payment

Demo Client AG pays a monthly service fee of 2,400 EUR net. Invoices are due within 14 calendar days.

## Termination

Either party may terminate the contract with 30 days written notice. Termination for cause remains possible without notice.

## Confidentiality

Both parties must keep confidential information secret for three years after the contract ends.
