# Sample Product Manual

## Product

The Acme Air Monitor A100 measures indoor air quality, temperature, humidity, and carbon dioxide levels.

## Battery

The device has a rechargeable battery with a typical runtime of 18 hours. A full charge takes about 2.5 hours via USB-C.

## Calibration

The carbon dioxide sensor should be calibrated every six months. Calibration takes about five minutes and must be done in fresh outdoor air.

## Connectivity

The monitor supports Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. Data export is available as CSV through the companion app.
